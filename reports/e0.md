## Probe fidelity and how to read these tables

**Correction (2026-11-23).** The first E0 tables used a `local_phase` that lost about half the power in every band (overlap-add bug; registry `correction_e0_local_phase`). It was fixed with a regression test and every E0 evaluation was rerun; only the `local_phase` cells changed (real, global_phase and flatten_tilt are identical in all 150 files). The pre-fix report is kept as reports/e0_PREFIX_local_phase_bug.md.

Reading the effects:
- `local_phase` keeps band power within about 0.03-0.04 log10 from 1 Hz up (0.09 below 1 Hz, limited by its 2 s window). It does **not** keep the 1 s short-time spectrum, so its cost includes reliance on the timing of spectral events at sub-2 s scale (spindle and K-complex onsets), not only on waveform shape within a cycle.
- Every surrogate also moves the input away from the training distribution. `flatten_tilt` keeps phase exactly and still collapses both models, so part of any surrogate's cost is fragility to unfamiliar input, not reliance on what it destroys. The effects are upper bounds on reliance.
- G1 is decided by the pre-registered rule (D-005), which these caveats do not change; its margin (smallest qualifying effect several points, intervals far from 0) is large relative to the fidelity errors above.
