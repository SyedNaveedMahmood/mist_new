Seeds [0]; each arm's 5 Sleep-EDF cv fold models (trained on Fpz-Cz, frozen recipe) evaluated on the **development part** (train + val people) of each external split, on the channel chosen per recording by the D-017 rule. External holdouts stay closed. These numbers change no phase-1 choice.
